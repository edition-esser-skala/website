To mark the tenth anniversary of our musicological work, we have established a [registered sole proprietorship](/contact.qmd) for our publishing activities. For our customers, everything remains the same – we continue to pursue our main goal of preserving 18th-century sacred music as cultural heritage, and therefore our editions remain free! Nevertheless, we will continue to appreciate your financial support, e.g., in the form of publishing commissions, performance license agreements, or donations.

We have also *revised our website* to comply with the new legal requirements. Notably, all scores are now directly stored on the server, which will enable archiving of the complete site including all editions.

Finally, the *EES Tools* have been updated to version 2026.01.0, which includes many exciting [new features](https://github.com/edition-esser-skala/ees-tools/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md).
